# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome here: to report a problem, ask a question, suggest something or send a fix.

## Please

- Be kind and patient, with me and with each other.
- Stay on topic: the app and how to make it better.
- Keep personal details out. Don't post account numbers, balances or bank messages, yours or anyone else's, without
  changing the numbers first.

## Not OK

- Insults, harassment, or discrimination of any kind.
- Spam and advertising.
- Sharing someone else's personal information.

## If something goes wrong

I'll edit or delete comments and issues that break these rules, and block anyone who keeps doing it. If someone is
bothering you here, use **Report content** from the comment's **⋯** menu to tell GitHub.
