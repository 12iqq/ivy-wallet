**Install:** download `IvyWalletUAE-v1.2.0.apk` below and open it. It updates the app you already have and keeps your
data. On a Windows computer, install `IvyWalletUAE-Desktop-v1.2.0.msi`; it replaces the earlier version. If Windows
says it protected your PC, click **More info → Run anyway**.

### New icon
Green fading into red inside the Ivy logo, with a light outline, on white: the colours of the UAE flag.

### Windows app
- Charts have amounts down the side. Point at a month to see its numbers: income, spending and what you saved, or
  your total balance and how it changed from the month before. On Reports, point at a day to see the running total.
- A short line under each chart and section says what it shows, and the **ⓘ** next to the numbers at the top explains
  how each one is worked out.
- Each category shows how many transactions it has, and the change from last month also shows last month's total.
- Transactions shows what you spent and received in total for what you searched, so searching "fee" tells you what
  fees cost you.
- **Export CSV** on every page saves what it shows, for Excel or Google Sheets.
- It finds your backup by itself. Save the phone's automatic backup in a Google Drive folder called `ivywallet-uae`,
  and the app reads the newest backup in it. You still need Google Drive for desktop on the computer, and you can
  still choose a file yourself.

### Phone
- **Settings → Automatic backup** suggests the name `ivywallet-uae.zip` and mentions the folder. If your backup is
  saved somewhere else now, tap **Backup file** and save it in an `ivywallet-uae` folder in Google Drive, so the
  Windows app finds it.
