**Install:** download `IvyWalletUAE-v1.0.10.apk` below and open it. It updates the app you already have and keeps your data.

### Card bills become planned payments
- ADCB's monthly billing alert ("Billing alert: Total due … Due date …") now becomes a planned payment into the card
  for the total due, due on the bill's due date, so it waits under Upcoming. Its description shows the total and the
  minimum due.
- It's paid from the account you paid that card from last time (else the one you paid another card from).
- When auto-add adds your payment into the card, the planned one is marked paid. A smaller payment lowers what's left,
  and "Paid AED …" is added to its description. ADCB sometimes sends the alert after you've already paid, so a payment
  from up to 2 days before the alert counts too.
- A new statement replaces last month's planned payment if that one is still unpaid, since the new total includes it.
- The card has to be linked in Auto-add transactions. To pick up the bill that's due now, tap **Scan last 30 days of
  SMS** there. On by default; switch it off with **Card bills become planned payments** in the same screen.
- A card payment you add by hand doesn't mark it paid: tap Pay on the planned one instead.

### Recurring charges
- New in Reports: **Recurring charges**. It finds the charges in your expenses that come back every week, month,
  three months or year, by their name: bills, subscriptions, fees. A shop you just visit often isn't one.
- Each shows its price (or a bill's usual amount), what it costs a month, when the next one is about due, a price
  change ("Up from …"), and whether it's already in your planned payments.
- Ones that are late past their schedule are listed under **Not seen lately**, for example a subscription you
  cancelled.
- Tap one to see its last charges. **Plan it** makes it a recurring planned payment from its next date; **Not
  recurring** hides it.
