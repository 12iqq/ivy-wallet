**Install:** download `IvyWalletUAE-v1.0.8.apk` below and open it. It updates the app you already have and keeps your data.

### Account tag on the right
- New in Settings → Transaction look: **Account on the right**. The category tag stays on the left and the
  account tag moves to the right end of the row, so the two read as separate things instead of a pair of
  look-alike tags.
- Each tag keeps to one line; a long name is shortened with "…".
- Works with the account colors and both tag text sizes, and the preview shows it in light and dark mode.
