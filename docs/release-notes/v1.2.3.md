**Install:** on your Windows computer, open `IvyWalletUAE-Desktop-v1.2.3.msi` below and click **Update**. The phone
app is the same as in 1.2.0. It's here too so both apps have the same version number.

### Windows app
- **Connect Google Drive.** The app gets your backup straight from Google Drive, without Google Drive for desktop or
  downloading anything. Click **Connect Google Drive**, sign in with the Google account your phone saves its backup to,
  and choose your backup in Google's file picker. From then on the app gets each new backup by itself, within a minute
  of it reaching Google Drive.
  - It can only see the backup you choose, nothing else in your Google Drive, and only reads it.
    [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/12iqq/ivy-wallet/blob/main/PRIVACY.md) says exactly what it connects to.
  - **Change backup** chooses another file, and **Disconnect Drive** signs the app out of Google.
  - Without internet, it keeps showing the last backup it got, and says there may be a newer one.
  - The `ivywallet-uae` folder and choosing a file yourself work as before.
- The first page doesn't repeat its buttons in the sidebar anymore.
