**Install:** download `IvyWalletUAE-v1.0.2.apk` below and open it. It updates the app you already have and keeps your data.

### Battery
- **Check for new messages on a schedule.** In Settings → Auto-add, choose *As they arrive* (as before) or every
  1, 3, 6, 12 or 24 hours. On a schedule the app stays closed: Android doesn't wake it for each SMS. New bank
  messages are read from your inbox on that schedule, and whenever you open the app.
- **The Google Wallet listener is switched off when it isn't needed.** Android keeps it connected and passes it
  every notification from every app, so it now only runs while *Google Wallet payments* is on and messages are
  checked as they arrive.

### Notifications
- **New Settings → Notifications screen.** Turn each kind on or off: detected bank transactions, card payment
  reminders, budget alerts, the 8 PM "Did you spend today?" reminder, and backup problems.
- "Reminders & backups" in Settings is now **Automatic backup**.
