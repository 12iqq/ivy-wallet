**Install:** download `IvyWalletUAE-v1.0.4.apk` below and open it. It updates the app you already have and keeps your data.

### Paying off a credit card
- Make a normal transfer (from your debit account to the card). As soon as the card is picked as **To**, the transfer
  panel shows **Paying off this card** with what the card owes and what's left after this payment
  (e.g. "AED 50.00 owed · AED 5.00 left after this"). **Pay all** fills in the full amount.
- Card payments don't ask for a category or tags any more: it's just money moving between your accounts.
