**Install:** download `IvyWalletUAE-v1.0.7.apk` below and open it. It updates the app you already have and keeps your data.

### SEWA bills become planned payments
- When a SEWA bill text arrives, it's added as a planned payment for the bill's total, due on the bill's due date,
  so it waits under Upcoming. It's named, categorised and paid from the account the way you paid the last one.
- When SEWA texts "We have received AED …" for your account, that payment is marked paid with the amount you paid.
  The bank's text for the same payment joins it instead of being added a second time, whichever arrives first, and
  it tells the app which card or account paid.
- The bill that's due now gets its planned payment the next time you open Reports. Bills already past their due
  date don't.
- On by default when utility bills are on; switch it off in Reports → Utility bills.

### Transaction look
- New in Settings: **Transaction look**.
- **Color account tags:** account tags use each account's color, like category tags. The text turns black or white
  so it stays readable on any color.
- **Text size** for account tags and for category tags: Small, Default, Large or Extra large. Account names in
  transfers follow the account tag size.
- The preview shows your own latest transaction in light and dark mode.
