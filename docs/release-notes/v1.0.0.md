First release of **Ivy Wallet UAE Edition**, a fork of the open-source Ivy Wallet.

**Install:** download `IvyWalletUAE-v1.0.0.apk` below. If Play Protect blocks it, tap *More details → Install anyway*.
Back up your old Ivy Wallet first (Settings → Backup data), then import the backup here.

### New
- **Credit card accounts**: limit, usage bar, statement and payment due days, "Spent this statement".
- **Auto-add transactions** from bank SMS (DIB, ADCB, Emirates Islamic, Emirates NBD, Liv, FAB, Mashreq and more) and,
  optionally, Google Wallet tap-to-pay notifications. Everything is processed on the phone.
- **Card / account endings** on accounts so detected transactions go to the right account.
- **Reports**: month/year, spending/income, category donut, spending pace, top places, past months, total balance.
- **Split payments** for Tabby / Tamara.
- **Quick-add shortcuts** learned from your history.
- **Reminders & backups**: card due reminders, budget alerts at 80% / 100%, automatic daily or weekly backup.

### Fixed
- Planned payments now show up reliably (including planned transfers).
- The daily "log your transactions" reminder works again.
- Deleted one-time / installment payments no longer come back.

### Removed
- The original project's Telegram, share, rating and poll prompts.
