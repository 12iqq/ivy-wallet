**Install:** download `IvyWalletUAE-v1.0.6.apk` below and open it. It updates the app you already have and keeps your data.

### Utility bills (SEWA)
- New and optional: the **Utility bills** card at the bottom of Reports. Turn on *Read SEWA bills from SMS* and the
  app reads every SEWA bill text on your phone, old ones too.
- **Latest bill:** electricity, water, gas, municipality fee and VAT, with the change since the bill before.
- **Bill by bill** chart of the last 12 bills, averages per service, this year's and all-time totals, and every bill
  in a list.
- Bills are kept, and included in backups, even after the texts are deleted. A new bill shows up the next time you
  open Reports.
- SEWA's texts give amounts only, not kWh or gallons, so the report shows what each service cost.

### Also
- *Delete all user data* also removes the stored utility bills.
