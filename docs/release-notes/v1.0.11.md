**Install:** download `IvyWalletUAE-v1.0.11.apk` below and open it. It updates the app you already have and keeps your data.

### Search
- Planned payments you haven't paid yet no longer show up in search results, so what you're looking for comes first.
  Paid ones still show, like any other transaction.
- Tap **Home** in the bottom bar while you're already on Home to open search, instead of reaching for the menu at the
  top. The search bar in that menu still works too.

### Settings
- **Colorful account labels** is gone from Advanced features. It was the same switch as **Color account tags** in
  Settings → Transaction look, which keeps it, and your setting stays as it was.
- Shorter descriptions under **Auto-add transactions** and **Find duplicates**.
