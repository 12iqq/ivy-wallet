**Install:** download `IvyWalletUAE-v1.0.5.apk` below and open it. It updates the app you already have and keeps your data.

### Bank texts read properly
- **Dubai Islamic Bank:** account payments (SEWA, Etisalat, cheques, transfers, fees), salary, cash deposits and
  older card texts ("AED: 39.00") are now read. Card purchases get the shop's real name; they were all called
  "A/C No" before, which mixed up the learned names.
- **Emirates Islamic** texts are recognised now (they were being ignored).
- **ADCB:** bills paid from the card ("for SEWA") and reversals are read, and shop names come without the city.
- **Tabby:** only Tabby Cash spends, top-ups and money sent or received count. Pay-later orders, reminders and
  "confirm in the app" texts are skipped; the instalments show up on the card that pays them.
- Payment reminders, card statements and payments waiting for approval are skipped.

### Card payments are transfers
- Paying a credit card from your bank sends two texts (the bank's "Card Payment" and the card's "payment
  received"). They now become one transfer from the bank to the card instead of a spend and an income. The same
  goes for Tabby Cash top-ups from your card.
- A card payment whose second text hasn't arrived yet waits in Review instead of being added as a spend.
- In Review, transfers show **From → To**, and any item can be switched to or from a transfer.

### Smarter names
- Auto-add uses the name and category you used last time at the same shop, preferring the same amount (each
  rent cheque, your salary).
- For a shop it hasn't seen yet, it uses your own name for it if one fits ("Nesto" for NESTO CENTER LLC).
