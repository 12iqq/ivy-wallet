**Install:** on your Windows computer, open `IvyWalletUAE-Desktop-v1.2.2.msi` below and click **Update**. The phone
app is the same as in 1.2.0. It's here too so both apps have the same version number.

### Windows app
- Opening the installer when the app is already on your computer says so, and asks what you want to do:
  - with an older version, **Update** puts the new one in its place and **Uninstall** removes the app;
  - with the same version, **Repair** puts back any of its files that are missing or broken and **Uninstall** removes
    it.

  From this version on, it also says which version you have.
- **Uninstall Ivy Wallet UAE.exe** is in the app's folder, next to **Ivy Wallet UAE.exe**
  (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Ivy Wallet UAE`). It does the same as **Uninstall Ivy Wallet UAE** in the Start menu: Windows asks
  if you're sure, then removes the app. Your backups stay where they are.
