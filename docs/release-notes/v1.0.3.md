**Install:** download `IvyWalletUAE-v1.0.3.apk` below and open it. It updates the app you already have and keeps your data.

### Auto-add learns your names for shops
- The **title** starts as the bank's name for the shop made readable: `GALADARI ICE CREAM CO LLC` becomes
  "Galadari Ice Cream". Rename it once, to "Baskin Robbins" say, and every later payment at that shop gets your
  title and category automatically. It learns from edits in the review list and in the normal transaction editor.
- The review list now shows the suggested title, so you rarely have to type one.

### Your description is yours again
- Auto-add no longer writes the bank's name into the **description**. The bank's wording for the shop is kept
  separately and shown under the description when you open a transaction ("From your bank: …").
- Descriptions that auto-add filled in before ("Emirates Islamic ··1234") are cleared, unless you had edited them.
