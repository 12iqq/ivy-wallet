**Install:** download `IvyWalletUAE-v1.0.3.apk` below and open it. It updates the app you already have and keeps your data.

### Auto-add learns your names for shops
- A detected transaction's **description** is now the bank's name for the shop (for example
  `GALADARI ICE CREAM CO LLC`) instead of the bank's name. The account already shows which bank it was.
- The **title** starts as that name made readable ("Galadari Ice Cream"). Rename it once, to "Baskin Robbins"
  say, and every later payment at that shop gets your title and category automatically. It learns from edits
  in the review list and in the normal transaction editor.
- The review list now shows the suggested title, so you rarely have to type one.
