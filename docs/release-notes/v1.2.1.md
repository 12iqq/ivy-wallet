**Install:** on your Windows computer, install `IvyWalletUAE-Desktop-v1.2.1.msi` below; it updates the app you have.
The phone app is the same as in 1.2.0. It's here too so both apps have the same version number.

### Windows app
- **All time.** Reports has **Month**, **Year** and **All time**: every category, place and transaction since your
  first one, with a bar for each month. On the Overview, switch between **Last 12 months** and **All time** at the top.
  With more than a year and a half of history, income and spending show a bar for each year.
- **Highlights**, a new page of fun facts about everything you've tracked:
  - where most of your money goes, where you go most often and where you spent the most;
  - your biggest purchase, and your priciest, cheapest and best-saving months;
  - the day of the week you spend the most, how many days you spent nothing, and what a typical purchase costs;
  - each year side by side, and your top categories.
- **Fuller CSV files.**
  - Transactions, from Reports or the Transactions page, now have the weekday and month, money in or out as plus and
    minus, whether you paid by card, the bank's name for the shop, tags, and whether it was a planned payment.
  - The Overview's file has a row for each month: income, spending, what you saved and the savings rate, the balance,
    how many transactions, the biggest category and top place, then what you spent on each category and with each
    account.
  - Highlights saves the same for each year.
- Bars stay slim when the window is wide.
